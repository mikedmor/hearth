Hearth

A home's own start page

Hearth runs on a home server, on the home's own network, for the people who live there. It shows the home's services, a page for each person, and each person's calendar.

Why this page exists

When someone in the home connects their Google Calendar to Hearth, Google asks for a public page about the app and its privacy policy. This is that page. Hearth itself is not on the internet and can't be reached from here.

Signing in with Google brings you back through this site's return page, which only sends your browser home to Hearth.

Privacy policy · Terms of service

Hearth is run by the household that uses it. This site has no tracking and no cookies.
