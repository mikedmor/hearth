For Hearth, a home's own start page, and its use of Google Calendar.

What Hearth is

Hearth is free, open-source software that a household runs on its own server, for the people who live there. It isn't a service run by a company: there are no accounts with anyone, nothing to buy, and no one but that household operates it.

Who it's for

Each copy of Hearth is for the household that runs it and the people they let use it. If you use someone else's Hearth (your family's, say), the person who runs it decides what it does and who can see what, and is the one to ask about it.

Using it with Google

Connecting a Google account is your choice, and you can undo it at any time: disconnect it in Hearth, or remove Hearth's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Hearth only reads the calendars you choose, and only changes events when you do so in Hearth. What it does with your data is described in the privacy policy. Google's own terms still apply to your Google account.

Use it fairly

Use Hearth for your own household, on calendars and services you're allowed to use. Don't use it to reach other people's accounts or devices without their permission.
